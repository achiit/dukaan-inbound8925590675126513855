Our story

Started by my father, kept by us

My father, Late Shri Mohan Lal Gupta, opened this shop in 1987 with one kadhai and one table. I was ten then, and my job was to sit and stir the sugar syrup. I still remember the smell of ghee at five in the morning.

My uncle and my elder brother worked beside him. Today my son helps at the counter. We still use the same recipes, and we still taste every batch before it goes on the shelf.

If something is not right, we do not sell it. That is how our customers became our neighbours, and our neighbours became family.
